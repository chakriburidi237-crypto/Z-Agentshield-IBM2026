Z-AgentShield | Local security-gateway prototype01
01 | The challenge

Agent actions need a control point

What changes with autonomous agents?

  • Agents can propose many tool and data operations at machine speed.
  • Individual services may apply different authorization and logging rules.
  • High-impact actions need consistent policy decisions and review routing.

What the prototype addresses

  • Evaluate proposed actions against ordered enterprise policy rules.
  • Record a decision and selected redacted evidence in SQLite.
  • Present history, pending reviews, and policy previews in one local console.
Scope: the gateway evaluates actions. It does not execute downstream operations or connect to enterprise systems.

04 | Security and transparency

Controls first; limitations shown

Implemented safeguards

  • Mandatory deny rules and default-deny policy behavior.
  • Policy ALLOW is blocked unless a trusted resource classifier verifies the target.
  • Selected sensitive parameter keys and inline values are redacted.
  • Swagger/OpenAPI is disabled by default and enabled only in explicit development mode.
  • Dashboard review actions remain read-only without trusted reviewer authentication.

Not claimed

  • No production identity provider or trusted reviewer authentication.
  • No authoritative resource registry/classifier configured by default.
  • Redaction is heuristic; SQLite is not cryptographically immutable.
  • Event records do not identify synthetic versus operational provenance.
  • No IBM service integration or verified LinuxONE deployment.
